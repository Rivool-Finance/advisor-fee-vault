What are you building, and who is it for

The advisor is the customer; the client is the user

  • Customer: Brazilian financial advisors and advisory offices. End users: their clients, high-net-worth and mostly not crypto users.
  • Their problem: no way to offer onchain dollar yield without holding client funds or sending clients to self-serve.
  • What was built: a Solana program. The client deposits USDC; yield comes from an existing vault (Kamino, or a stand-in); the office and advisor fees plus Rivool's 15% of yield are charged and split onchain; only the client can withdraw.
  • New for the advisor: fees enforced by code, paid automatically, no billing work, the client keeps custody.
  • New for the client: every fee and the net yield visible onchain.
  • Cut from the old draft: "curate the best on-chain products", "legacy shelf".
