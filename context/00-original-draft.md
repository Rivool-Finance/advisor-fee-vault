# Original draft (as shared 2026-10-09)

The team's first draft of the Colosseum project details, kept for reference. Superseded by
the direction in `01-decisions-and-findings.md` and the outline in `../submission-outline.html`.

PROJECT NAME: Rivool Finance

BRIEF DESCRIPTION: Rivool is a technology platform that lets financial advisors offer the best
on-chain products on Solana to their clients. Self-custodial for the client, fee-based for the advisor.

PROJECT WEBSITE: https://rivool.finance

WHAT ARE YOU BUILDING, AND WHO IS IT FOR? Platform for advisors to offer curated on-chain
products (starting with yield vaults) in a few clicks; client self-custodial; advisor earns a fee
settled onchain. For advisors and their qualified and high-net-worth clients.

WHY NOW: lived the problem; fee-based beats commissions; Solana speed and cost; stablecoin and RWA
adoption; "validated the model with live vaults on Morpho".

TECHNOLOGIES: Rust/Anchor, TypeScript/React, Phantom, Solflare, Solana yield protocols to confirm
(Kamino, Drift, MarginFi), Morpho (EVM) as the model, GitHub, Claude Code.

CHAINS: Solana. CATEGORY: DeFi. MOBILE: No. LOCATION: Brazil.

NOTES FOR JUDGES: live vaults on Morpho predate the hackathon; Solana work is net-new.

MEDIA AND CODE: logo, GitHub (Solana build), repo context, demo video (3 min), pitch video,
X profile https://x.com/rivool_finance.
