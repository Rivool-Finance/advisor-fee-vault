Build on Solana

The advisor fee vault

What we already run

Our fee rules live in the API, and the client has to trust them

The fee-based model works today, but it is enforced offchain: accrued daily in our database, collected at withdrawal, reconciled by indexers.

AFTERIndexer reconciles the ledger

Advisory fee on managed balance, office part + advisor partUp to 1% a yearapps/api/src/config/aum-fee.ts:12

The insight. We built and debugged this machine. Holding the same rules in a program makes them checkable by the client and the advisor, removes the reconciliation step, and pays the office and the advisor without Rivool in the middle.

Why this product

Dollar yield is the only high-fit option that earns on every dollar, every year

The vault charges the advisory fee and Rivool's performance fee in the same program, on a product every client can use. Stocks bring large balances; yield is where Rivool earns on them.

Per US$100k client, per yearRivoolAdvisor and officeWho can buy
Dollar yield vault (Kamino USDC, later USDY)15% of realised yield: about US$600 to 1,200Up to 1%: up to US$1,000Every client
Ondo stocks and ETFs on SolanaUS$0.05 per trade, plus a proposed 0 to 1%; near zero for buy and holdUp to 1%: up to US$1,000Qualified investors
Vault infrastructure and walletsNo direct revenue; lowers costNoneEnabler
Fee stack to settle before the pitch. At 4% yield, 15% for Rivool plus 1% for the advisor leaves the client about 2.4%; at 8% about 5.8%. Options: cap the advisory fee on yield-only balances, charge one fee or the other, and show the client's net yield in the demo.
Margin to check. On Base, yield goes through Deframe, which carries its own fee (deframe_fee in configuration). Composing with Kamino directly removes that layer; size unknown.

Illustrative: yield range is the 4 to 8% shown on rivool.finance, not a measured Solana rate. Fee rules from the fee model (DR-684) and the wiki commission note; stock fee proposal DR-651; advisor balances count stocks (#984).

Decisions and risks

What could sink the entry or the roadmap

Build capacity. The loop needs someone who can write and test an Anchor program by Sunday. Without that, the entry is a pitch with mockups, which is the pattern that does not win.
Incomplete disclosure. Naming only Morpho understates the live product. List everything that existed before 14 Sep.
Who may charge the fee. Fee-based advice in Brazil sits with registered consultants; judges will ask whether an assessor can charge it. Answer in one line.
Brazilian Central Bank rules (Res. 519 to 521). Reported compliance deadline for existing firms: 30 Oct 2026; stablecoin transfers to self-custody wallets fall under FX rules with wallet-owner identification. Read from a Notabene summary, not the BCB text. Take it to legal; it touches every chain.
Unverified pieces. Reflect on Solana mainnet, USDY terms for Brazil, Kamino permissionless vault creation, Rain settlement on Solana. Avoid Drift: secondary reports of an April 2026 exploit.
BCB summary: notabene.id/post/brazils-central-bank-regulates-virtual-asset-service-providers-what-bcb-resolutions-mean-for-crypto-compliance. Drift: coin360.com report, unverified.
